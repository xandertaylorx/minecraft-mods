Hero Arsenal

Four superhero weapons, each with two powers. Throw a hammer that calls down lightning, then catch it when it flies back.

Download v1.0.0 See the items

The Items

All in the Combat tab in creative mode.

Mjolnir

Epic
  • Hold Right-ClickWind it up over your head, then let go to throw. It calls lightning on whatever it hits, then flies back into your hand.
  • Sneak + Right-ClickCall lightning wherever you're looking, up to 64 blocks away.
  • Attack12 damage. Critical hits always call lightning.

Your own lightning never hurts you, and it never sets blocks on fire.

Star Shield

Epic
  • Hold Right-ClickBlock attacks like a normal shield. It never breaks.
  • Sneak + Right-ClickThrow it. It bounces from mob to mob and off walls (up to 4 bounces), then comes back.

Pulse Gauntlet

Epic
  • Right-ClickFire a 40-block energy beam that knocks mobs back and sets them on fire.
  • Sneak + Right-ClickThrusters launch you where you're looking. Chain them to fly.

No fall damage while it's in your hand.

Web Shooter

Rare
  • Right-Click a mobStick it in place with webs for 5 seconds.
  • Right-Click a blockSwing toward it.

No fall damage while it's in your hand.

FAQ

Does it work with Forge or NeoForge?

No, it's Fabric only for now.

Does it work in multiplayer?

Yes. Install it on the server and on every player's game.

Will the lightning burn down my house?

No. Hero Arsenal lightning never sets blocks on fire. Mobs still burn, though.

I threw Mjolnir with a full inventory. Where did it go?

It drops on the ground right next to you.
