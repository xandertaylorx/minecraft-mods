What you get

Every effect has its own switch in the shader settings.

God rays

Sunlight streams through gaps in the leaves as real beams of light, visible from any angle (High).

Lens flare

Look toward the sun for a soft glow, a streak and colored ghosts. Hidden when trees or hills block the sun.

Living atmosphere

Misty pink dawns, clear blue noons, golden-hazed sunsets and a faint blue mist at night.

Starry nights

Silvery-blue moonlight, a glowing halo around the moon and twinkling stars.

Soft shadows

Trees, buildings and you cast soft shadows that move with the sun and moon.

Living world

Grass, flowers, crops and leaves sway in the wind (more when it rains).

Shiny water

Rippling water that reflects the sky and blazes with the sun at sunset.

Glowing lights

Torches, lanterns, glowstone and lava glow, with soft bloom around bright things.

Mac tips

Blockshine only uses features Macs support, but these help a lot too.

Turn off Retina rendering

On a Retina or 4K screen, Minecraft draws 4× the pixels. Neither Sodium nor vanilla has a setting for this, so add our tiny Mac FPS Boost mod. It's the biggest speed-up on a Mac.

Pick Medium or Low

On older Intel Macs or MacBook Airs, Low keeps shadows but skips the extras. Potato has no shadows at all.

Keep render distance sensible

8–12 chunks looks great with Blockshine's fog. Shadows only cover the area near you either way.

Install Iris

Add Iris and Sodium to your Fabric instance's mods folder.

Add the pack

Put blockshine-1.0.0.zip in the shaderpacks folder. Don't unzip it.

Turn it on

Options → Video Settings → Shader Packs, pick Blockshine, then choose a profile (Potato to High).

FAQ

Which profile should I pick?

Start with Medium. If it stutters, go to Low; on a very old laptop, Potato (no shadows) is the fastest.

Does it work with OptiFine?

It's made and tested for Iris. Iris is the modern, faster option.

Why don't mobs cast shadows?

On purpose: skipping mob and chest shadows saves a lot of work every frame. You still cast your own shadow.

Does it work in the Nether and the End?

Yes, with their own lighting and fog. Shadows switch off there automatically since there's no sun.
