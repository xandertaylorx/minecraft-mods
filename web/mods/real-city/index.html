Real City

Type /setcity Manchester, UK and your world follows that city: the real time, sunrise and sunset, moon phase and weather, live.

Download v1.0.0 See how it works

Your world, somewhere real

Pick any city on Earth. The screenshots here are the real thing: drizzle in Tokyo at 4am, sunshine in Los Angeles at 11am, dawn in Sydney.

Real time

Minecraft's sunrise is the city's sunrise and Minecraft's sunset is its sunset, so long summer days and short winter days show up in the sky.

Real weather

Clear, rain or thunderstorm, straight from the city's current conditions. Snow and drizzle count as rain.

Real moon

The 8 Minecraft moon phases match the real moon. Full moon outside means a full moon in real life.

Weather comes from Open-Meteo, free and with no account or key. It refreshes every 10 minutes.

Commands

In singleplayer anyone can use them. On a server, /setcity and /realcity need op.

/setcity <city>Picks the city. If several cities share a name, add the country or state: /setcity Manchester, UK, /setcity Paris, Texas, or just /setcity Los Angeles.
/cityShows the city, its local time, weather, temperature, wind, sunrise and sunset, and the moon phase. Anyone can use it.
/city hudTurns your own action bar on or off: city, time, temperature and weather above your hotbar.
/realcity on | offTurns syncing on or off. Off gives you normal Minecraft time and weather back.
/realcity time | weatherSync only the sky, or only the rain and thunder: /realcity weather off.
/realcity announceTell chat when the real weather changes, like "It's started raining in Manchester".
/realcity refreshFetch the weather again right now.

FAQ

Does the day still last 20 minutes?

No. The sky follows the real clock, so a Minecraft day lasts a real day. The sun creeps across the sky and the world is dark when it's really dark. If you'd rather have normal days, use /realcity time off and keep just the weather.

Can I sleep through the night?

You can try, but the sky snaps back to the real time within 5 seconds. Torches and beds are still useful for spawn points.

Does it work in multiplayer?

Yes. Install it on the server only. Everyone sees the same city.

What if my internet drops?

The last weather keeps going for 15 minutes, the clock keeps running, and the mod retries every minute.

What happens if I remove the mod?

If syncing was on, your world's clock is left paused. Run /realcity off before you remove it and time flows normally again.
