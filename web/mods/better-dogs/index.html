Better Dogs

Sneak + right-click your dog to open its settings. Make it invincible, have it fetch dropped loot for you, guard you from mobs and more.

Download v1.0.0 See the settings

Every dog gets a settings screen

Hold sneak and right-click a dog you've tamed. Settings are saved on each dog, so every dog can be set up differently. Normal right-click still makes it sit or stand.

InvincibleNothing can hurt your dog: mobs, lava, fire, falling, drowning or explosions. Only /kill and the void still work.
Auto-collectYour dog picks up dropped items near you and brings them straight to your inventory. Choose Off, Food only, Good stuff or Everything.
BehaviorDefend: a normal dog. Guard: also hunts any hostile mob that comes near you (but leaves creepers alone). Passive: never fights.
RegenerationHeals half a heart every 2 seconds.
Safe from youYour own swings, arrows and explosions can't hurt your dog by accident. On by default.
Swift pawsRuns 40% faster, so it keeps up when you sprint.
GlowOutlined through walls so you never lose it.
Danger alertBarks and makes hostile mobs near you glow for a few seconds, even behind walls.
Name, collar, sit, callRename it without a name tag, pick any collar color, make it sit or stand, or teleport it to you with Call to me.

Fetch modes

Your dog only picks up items within about 20 blocks of you, and never takes things you threw away yourself.

Food only

Meat, fish, bread, apples and anything else you can eat. Rotten flesh, spider eyes and poisonous potatoes are skipped.

Good stuff

Food, plus ores, gems, ingots, coal, redstone, mob drops like bones, string, gunpowder and leather, tools, armor, enchanted books and rare items. It skips dirt, cobblestone, seeds and other junk.

Everything

Every dropped item near you. Handy after mining or a big fight.

The screen also shows your dog's health and how many items it has fetched and mobs it has defeated.

FAQ

Can other players change my dog?

No. Only the owner can open a dog's settings. Everyone else gets "That's not your dog."

Where do fetched items go if my inventory is full?

They're dropped at your feet, and your dog won't keep fetching them.

Does it work in multiplayer?

Yes. Install it on the server and on every player's game.

What happens if I remove the mod?

Your dogs go back to being normal dogs. Nothing breaks.
