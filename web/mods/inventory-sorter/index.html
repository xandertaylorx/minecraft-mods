Inventory Sorter

One click and your chest or backpack sorts itself: stacks merged, related items side by side.

Download v1.3.2 How it works

How it works

Open any chest, barrel, ender chest, shulker box or your own inventory and look for the little sort icon (three bars, long to short).

The icon button

Chests get two: the top one sorts the chest, the one on the "Inventory" row sorts your backpack. In your own inventory screen it sits under the crafting grid. Hover for a tooltip.

Quick stack to nearby chests

In your own inventory there is a second icon, an arrow dropping into a chest. Click it and every item in your main inventory (not the hotbar) that a chest or barrel within 4 blocks already holds is sent there, nearest chest first, like Terraria. In singleplayer (and when you host a LAN world) it is instant: the items are moved directly and nothing opens. On a multiplayer server it has to open chests, so it remembers what is inside every chest or barrel you have opened and only visits the ones that hold something you are carrying (open a chest once to teach it). The chest screens are hidden while it works.

Or press R

R sorts the open chest (or your inventory if none is open). Shift + R always sorts your own 27 inventory slots. The hotbar and armor are never touched. Rebind it in Controls.

Smart order

Partial stacks are merged first, then everything follows the creative inventory order, so stone sits with stone bricks, tools with tools and food with food.

Safe on servers

It only uses ordinary slot clicks (3 per tick, so no spam kicks), and it is client-side only, so nothing needs installing on the server.
