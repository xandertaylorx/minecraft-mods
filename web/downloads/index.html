After you download

Mods (.jar)

Put the .jar in your Fabric instance's mods folder, next to Fabric API.

Shader packs (.zip)

Put the .zip in the shaderpacks folder and pick it in Iris's shader settings.

Check the version

The Minecraft version and loader need to match your instance, or the game won't load the mod.
